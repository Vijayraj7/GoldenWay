Getting Started

How to Join & Get Started

Becoming a member is simple — follow these 6 easy steps

1

Get Your Referral Link from a Sponsor

Every new member must be invited by an existing GoldenWay member. Ask your sponsor for their personal referral link. This link automatically places you in their Left or Right team.

2

Register Your Account

Click the referral link and fill in your details — name, email, phone number, and a secure password. Your sponsor's information is captured automatically.

3

Verify Your Email

An activation email will be sent to you. Click the confirmation link in the email to activate your account. This protects your account and ensures only you can access it.

4

Login to Your Dashboard

Once activated, log in using your email and password. You will see your personal dashboard with your wallet, community tree, income summary, and all your account details.

5

Add USDT to Your Wallet

Deposit USDT into your GoldenWay wallet. You can send from any crypto exchange or wallet to your unique GoldenWay deposit address shown in your dashboard.

6

Subscribe to a Plan & Start Earning!

Use your wallet balance to subscribe to a membership plan. Once subscribed, you are now an Active Member — all income streams activate and your earnings begin immediately.

💡 Tip: The sooner you subscribe and start building your team, the faster your income grows. Your Left and Right team businesses directly determine your daily binary income — balance both sides for maximum reward.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is USDT and why do we use it?
USDT (Tether) is a stable digital currency that is always equal to 1 US Dollar. It never loses value like other cryptocurrencies. GoldenWay uses USDT so your earnings are always stable, predictable, and globally accessible.
❓ How much do I need to start?
You can start with any subscription amount. The more you invest, the higher your binary volume and stake income. Our earnings calculator on the website can show you projections before you commit.
❓ When do I receive my daily income?
Binary income and staking income are credited automatically to your account every day. Referral and level incomes are credited instantly the moment your downline subscribes to a plan — no waiting.
❓ What is the 2× Return Cap?
The 2× cap means you are guaranteed to earn double your total subscription investment. For example, if you subscribe with 500 USDT, you will earn 1,000 USDT total across all income types. This makes the platform fair and sustainable for everyone.
❓ Do I need to refer people to earn?
You earn staking income (0.5%/day) even without referring anyone. However, referring members greatly increases your binary income and level income — the more your team grows, the more you earn every day.
❓ How do I withdraw my earnings?
Go to your dashboard, click Withdraw, enter the amount and your USDT wallet address, and submit. Our team processes and approves withdrawal requests promptly — typically within 24 hours.
